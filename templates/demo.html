Nepal brings together some of the planet's most dramatic mountain landscapes with ancient cities, sacred places, diverse communities and warm lowland wilderness.

Walk beneath Himalayan giants, discover living heritage in the Kathmandu Valley, travel into the landscapes of Mustang or slow down among forests and rivers in the Terai.

With Yeti Adventure, your Nepal journey can follow a classic route or take a more personal direction. Tell us what matters to you and we will help shape the experience around it.
