Tradition, Grandeur and
Extraordinary Diversity.

India is a continent disguised as a country. Home to more than a billion people, it brings together imperial palaces, sacred rivers, ancient pilgrimage routes, vibrant bazaars, tiger reserves and peaceful waterways into an extraordinary living tapestry.

From the desert citadels and royal forts of Rajasthan to the devotional morning ghats of Varanasi, the tea hills of Darjeeling and the tranquil backwaters of Kerala, every region presents a completely unique world.
