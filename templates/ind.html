The Roof of the World

A Realm of Grandeur and
Extraordinary Devotion.

Tibet travel is an invitation to explore one of the world’s most extraordinary spiritual destinations. Known as the Roof of the World and the Third Pole, Tibet evokes a sense of grandeur and devotion amid wild, high-altitude landscapes.

Across the Tibetan Plateau, immense valleys, alpine lakes and sacred mountains form the headwaters of many of Asia’s great rivers. None is more revered than Mount Kailash, a pilgrimage destination held sacred across several faiths.

Centuries of religious conviction and artistic tradition have shaped remarkable monuments throughout the region, including Norbulingka—the historic summer palace—and the iconic Potala Palace rising above Lhasa.
