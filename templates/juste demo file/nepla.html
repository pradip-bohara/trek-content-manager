A land of contrasts

Epic journeys in the heart of the Himalayas

Nestled between China and India, Nepal is a land of extraordinary contrasts—from the warm lowlands of the Terai to the towering snow-covered giants of the Himalayas. Home to eight of the world's ten highest mountains, including Mt. Everest, Nepal offers landscapes shaped for adventure and spiritual discovery.

Beyond its peaks, Nepal is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and home to ancient cities, monasteries, temples and a rich tapestry of cultures and traditions that continue to shape everyday life.

At Yeti Adventure, we create journeys around the way you want to experience Nepal. From Everest and Annapurna to remote valleys and lesser-travelled trails, our team helps you travel safely, responsibly and memorably.
