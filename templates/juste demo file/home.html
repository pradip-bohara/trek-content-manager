Not just another trek.

Yeti Adventure began in Kathmandu in 1999 as Himalayan Society Pvt. Ltd. The company became Yeti Adventure Pvt. Ltd. in 2011, carrying forward the same purpose: to create safe, experiential journeys through Nepal and the wider Himalaya.

We are a government-registered, Nepali-owned travel company and part of the Yeti World family, a group that supports more than 1,500 people. Our strength is not simply access to mountains. It is the lived knowledge, trusted relationships and careful judgement that come from operating here for decades.

Nepal is not a checklist. It is a feeling—built from quiet trails, living traditions and moments worth slowing down for.
