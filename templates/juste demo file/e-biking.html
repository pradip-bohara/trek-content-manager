A different way into Nepal

Let the motor help. Let the mountain lead.

E-biking changes the rhythm of a Himalayan journey. You still climb, steer, brake and feel the trail — but electric assistance gives you another reserve when the road turns steep or the altitude starts to bite.

Thamserku's e-biking story began with routes around the Kathmandu Valley and grew into longer journeys toward Mustang and Lo Manthang. Their first group to reach Lo Manthang by e-bike covered roughly 350 km across the region.

“The point is not to conquer the mountain. It is to have more time to experience it.”

The ride

From green hills to the high desert.

Nepal gives e-bike journeys a remarkable change of scenery. A route can begin among villages and forests and finish beside the dry cliffs, monasteries and wide-open valleys of Mustang.

04 · Upper Mustang

Samar, Charang & Lo Manthang

Ride into the rain-shadow landscape, past old villages and isolated trails, towards the walled city of Lo Manthang.

Why ride electric?

More mountain. More moments.

The best part of an e-bike is not going faster. It is having another option when the terrain gets demanding — so you can keep your attention on the landscape, the people and the trail ahead.

01

Climb with confidence

Electric assistance takes some pressure out of long gradients and uneven mountain roads.

02

Keep the group together

A little assistance can make mixed riding abilities work better on the same journey.

03

Save something for the descent

Arrive at the top with enough energy to enjoy the trail back down.

04

Travel deeper

With the right route and support, e-biking opens longer days and bigger landscapes to more riders.
