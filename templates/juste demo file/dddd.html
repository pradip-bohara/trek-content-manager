ABOUT YETI ADVENTURE

Welcome to Yeti Adventure!

Founded in 1999 as Himalayan Society Pvt. Ltd., we rebranded to Yeti Adventure Pvt. Ltd. in 2011. As a leading government-registered trekking and tour operator based in Kathmandu, we operate proudly under the Yeti World banner—employing over 1,500 dedicated staff across Nepal.
