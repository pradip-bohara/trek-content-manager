The Himalayan Perspective

Some journeys are felt, not simply travelled.

A helicopter journey through Nepal is more than a quick way to reach the mountains. It is the moment Kathmandu fades beneath you, green hills give way to rugged valleys, and the great Himalayan peaks slowly appear beyond the clouds.

From Everest and the Khumbu to Annapurna and Langtang, our helicopter experiences are designed for travellers who want to experience Nepal's extraordinary landscapes while making the most of their time.
