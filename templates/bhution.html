Gross National Happiness.

Tucked away in the Eastern Himalayas, Bhutan is a kingdom like no other. Steeped in Buddhist tradition and guided by the principle of Gross National Happiness, it offers travellers a rare blend of spiritual serenity, cultural richness and stunning alpine beauty. From the cliff-hanging Tiger’s Nest Monastery to dramatic Himalayan passes and quiet valley villages, Bhutan remains deeply authentic—and largely untouched.

Unlike anywhere else, Bhutan prioritises low-impact tourism. Visitor numbers are carefully managed, making each journey feel intimate and exclusive. Trek through rhododendron forests, witness sacred festivals and discover a pace of life guided by harmony and sustainability.

At Yeti Adventure, we offer immersive itineraries across Bhutan—from cultural highlights to high-altitude journeys such as the Jhomolhari and Snowman Treks. We take care of the formalities while you discover the soul of this mystical Himalayan kingdom.
